# Minimal Book Canon — revision canon-v1

Mara is a first-year bridge duelist with an injured left hand. The injury makes
closing that hand painful and weakens sustained grip.

Academy qualifying duels take place on segmented stone bridges. A clean ring-out
scores one point; rescue intervention pauses the bout but does not erase points.
Students may reinforce existing stone but may not create new material.

Mara's rival, Iven, can shift force through stone he is touching. He cannot use
the technique through a gap.
