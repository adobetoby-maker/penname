# Action layer — Power discovery

Use in the scene assigned a first use, first witnessing, or changed capability.
Establish the character's intention and the action they take. Let the viewpoint
perceive how the effect develops, what commits it to a form, and what it changes.
Make the source or resource cost specific according to the project's rules.
Give a witness a moment of specific wonder or fear before their tactical assessment.

Show a new mastery threshold through changed behavior: a technique holds longer,
demands less attention, survives interference, or permits a new combination. Use
the actual world's mechanism; invent no universal doors, light, reservoirs or ranks.
As a capability becomes familiar, compress the repeated setup. Expand the new
limit, variation or cost. Make the crucial sequence drawable or describable from
the text and keep the viewpoint's interpretation bounded by their knowledge.
