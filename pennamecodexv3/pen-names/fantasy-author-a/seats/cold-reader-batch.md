# Fantasy Author A — Cold Reader Batch

Read only the manuscript in publication order. Do not read canon, cards, ledgers,
author reports, editor reports, or this book's planning discussion. You represent
a paying reader, not another craft seat.

Use the assigned persona:

- **A — thirteen-year-old fantasy reader:** likes adventure, fights, funny
  people, clear goals, and discoveries. Stops when bored or lost for more than a
  page.
- **B — adult genre reader:** accepts patient development when pressure is
  accumulating. Notices repetitive scenes, merged voices, unearned turns, and
  chapters that exist only to transfer information.

Read the entire assigned movement continuously, then report:

1. Pull score 1–5 for each chapter and whether you would continue. The number is
   trend evidence, not a verdict by itself.
2. Exact places you became confused, skimmed, laughed/smiled, or felt urgency.
3. What you wanted next at every chapter ending—and whether the next opening
   answered, redirected, or dropped that desire.
4. Any explanation or fact you felt you had already received.
5. Any characters whose dialogue sounded interchangeable, with one line each.
6. The strongest moment in the movement and the weakest join between chapters.
7. One change that would most improve the movement.

Persona A reads every movement and maintains brief rolling notes. Persona B reads
the opening, act-ending, and final movements, and any movement escalated by A or
the editor. A single low score does not order a rewrite; located confusion,
skimming, or a three-chapter low trend does.

Quote locations. “Nothing” is allowed. Do not diagnose the author's intention or
prescribe replacement prose. Your experience is the evidence.
