# Monroe Jackson — Fantasy Design Provenance

This is an audit document. It is not loaded into a drafting prompt.

Fantasy Author A synthesizes craft mechanisms researched from four traditions:

- physically literate, terrain-driven combat and tempo control;
- rule-bound wonder, limitations, foreshadowing, and payoff architecture;
- measurable progression, tournament pressure, and social rank movement;
- character-facing system design, strong coping voice, serial discipline, and
  moral cost.

The full clean-room briefs remain the source of record:

- `../../research/salvatore-combat.md`
- `../../research/sanderson-craft.md`
- `../../research/ironprince-progression.md`
- `../../research/hwfwm-litrpg.md`

No published fiction was supplied to the runtime author. Influence names and
protected expression are excluded from `VOICE.md`. The profile preserves craft
mechanisms rather than reproducing any author's prose, characters, terminology,
or recognizable scene construction.

The public author identity is `Monroe Jackson — Fantasy`, author standard
`1.3.0`. The stable internal ID `fantasy-author-a` remains only for compatibility
with existing book packets and run records.

## Monroe 1.3 addendum (owner-directed revision)

An earlier pass (`1.1.1`/`1.2.0`) distilled these same four briefs into
craft-layer rules that, in at least one documented case (`KINETIC.md`'s
jargon-rationing rule), paired a real author's own stated method with a
*separate*, uncredited secondary source's opinion — narrowing the technique
into something more cautious than what the researched author actually does.
Monroe 1.3 corrects this: the craft-layer files (`craft/CORE.md`,
`craft/WORLDBUILDING.md`, `craft/action-layers/KINETIC.md`,
`craft/modules/progression.md`) were rewritten directly from the four primary
research briefs (`../../research/salvatore-combat.md`,
`../../research/sanderson-craft.md`, `../../research/ironprince-progression.md`,
`../../research/hwfwm-litrpg.md`) plus the later numeric cadence benchmark
(`../../research/fantasyau1.1/action-humor-framework.md` and its four
companion cadence studies), with no secondary-critic filtering. The cadence
floors from that benchmark pass — set-piece spacing, length, humor rhythm,
power visibility — are carried forward unchanged; they already push toward
more action density and volume, which is what prompted the benchmark research
in the first place.

The anti-pastiche firewall (`CORE.md` invariant 6) was narrowed by explicit
owner ruling in this same pass: style and technique are unrestricted and may
be channeled openly, by name, in the compiled runtime prompt — the owner's
own words were "writing style is not plagiarism... I would be much more
free about imitation." What stays off-limits is unchanged and absolute: a
specific author's verbatim wording, their named characters, and their
invented worlds/settings. The craft-layer files once again name Salvatore,
Sanderson, O'Connor & Chmilenko (*Iron Prince*), and Shirtaloon (*He Who
Fights With Monsters*) directly, and direct the drafting model to channel
their demonstrated technique — this is deliberate, not a regression. Adopting
a technique in full, even by name, is not the same as imitating a passage;
that line is what invariant 6 now states explicitly.
