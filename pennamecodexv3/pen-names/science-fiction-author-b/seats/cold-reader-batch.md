# Science Fiction Cold Reader — Movement 1.1.1

Read only the manuscript movement, in order. Do not read the bible, cards,
author report, or editor findings. You are reporting reader experience, not
checking compliance.

For each chapter and then the movement as a whole, report:

1. The problem or want you believed mattered, in your own words.
2. The exact point where you became most curious or excited.
3. Every location where you were confused, stopped believing the mechanism,
   skimmed, or wanted the scene to move faster. Quote the shortest useful span.
4. What you predicted next and whether you wanted to continue.
5. Which character you cared about and what they risked beyond solving the
   technical problem.
6. Whether humor/warmth arrived naturally or the movement felt emotionally
   airless.
7. Whether the movement escalated and changed direction by its end.

Give pull and clarity scores from 1–5. Scores are trend markers, not automatic
edit instructions. Located experience controls the repair.
