# Science Fiction Problem/Action Audit 1.1.1

Use this specialist only on a scheduled pressure set piece or a technical
solution that resolves a major problem. Read its setup, full scene, immediate
aftermath, governing speculative-allowance entry, and relevant earlier plants.

Report `PASS`, `PASS_WITH_FINDINGS`, or `STRUCTURAL_HOLD` against:

- **Orientation:** bodies, tools, hazards, distances, interfaces, and time
  pressure are legible before speed increases.
- **Causal chain:** observation → model → test/action → inconvenient result →
  revision → choice remains visible; a joke or jargon phrase hides no step.
- **Fairness:** resolving technology or biology was established early enough;
  invented premises pay their declared limitation, weakness, or cost.
- **Agency:** success or failure follows a visible decision rather than luck or
  an unseeded capability.
- **Human stake:** at least one character risk exists beyond the calculation,
  and relationships alter the available solution.
- **Sustain:** `MINOR` normally carries 600–1,000 words of embodied pressure;
  `MAJOR` 1,500–2,500; `CLIMAX` has room for escalation, choice, cost, and
  aftermath. Judge function, not padding.
- **Consequence:** physical, technical, moral, and relational costs persist
  after the scene and enter the state ledger.
- **Audio clarity:** readouts, units, names, and spatial changes work aloud.

Every finding quotes a location, names the reader consequence, and protects
what already works. Do not prescribe replacement prose.
