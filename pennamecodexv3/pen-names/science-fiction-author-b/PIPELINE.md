# Monroe Jackson — Science Fiction Movement Pipeline 1.1.1

This is the default production loop for `/scifiauthor`. It preserves the
humane problem-solving voice while giving Fable enough uninterrupted runway to
sustain causality, relationships, action, humor, and chapter-to-chapter pull.

## Default unit

Draft one contiguous movement of three to five chapters. Four is the default.
Use three around a dense discovery, moral choice, or major pressure set piece;
use five when the causal chain is sequential and the cards are already firm.

Before drafting, supply:

- approved cards for the complete movement;
- entry pressure, escalation, and movement turn in one sentence;
- canon, speculative-allowance table, state ledger, name registry, open MICE
  threads, and current plant/payoff map;
- the previous chapter and the first card after the movement;
- a schedule targeting seven or eight pressure set pieces per twenty chapters,
  with five as the hard floor and no gap longer than four chapters;
- set-piece scale marked `MINOR`, `MAJOR`, or `CLIMAX`, with higher chapter
  targets where the scene requires room.

A pressure set piece may be an EVA, chase, rescue, combat, dangerous experiment,
failure cascade, repair under a clock, survival sequence, or confrontation. It
must involve embodied risk and irreversible choices, not merely people talking
about a problem.

## Loop

1. **Movement brief.** Compile the cards into
   `seats/movement-brief.template.md`. Load the author identity once and read
   the movement as a causal unit.
2. **Draft forward.** Write the chapters once, in order. Chapter 1 receives two
   blind drafts automatically. Chapters 2–3 receive alternatives only when an
   opening reader locates a failure. Before each next chapter, update the
   ledger, read the completed chapter, and read the next card.
3. **Light close.** Between chapters check only card function, canon, state,
   speculative allowance, names, reader standard, word band, and whether the
   promised event was dramatized. Repair blockers; defer polish.
4. **Freeze the movement.** File one batch report. Do not invisibly revise the
   earlier chapters while later ones are under review.
5. **Batch edit.** The cross-family editor reads all chapters continuously for
   causal handoffs, state and knowledge, repeated explanations, relationship
   carryover, problem-engine progress, action spacing, set-piece scale, rolling
   humor, voice ownership, and pull.
6. **Cold read.** Reader A reads every movement. Reader B reads the opening,
   act turns, ending, and anything flagged by A or the editor. Scores are trend
   evidence; located confusion, disbelief, skimming, and delight govern repair.
7. **Specialist audit.** Run `seats/problem-action-audit.md` only for scheduled
   pressure set pieces or technical solutions that resolve a major problem.
   Independent verification is reserved for HIGH/BLOCKER, disputed evidence,
   real-world technical accuracy, or structural-rewrite proposals.
8. **One repair pass.** Repair one consolidated list in reading order. Preserve
   named strengths. Prefer a seam repair that fixes both chapters. Additions may
   total up to eight percent of a chapter's target across repair cycles before
   architecture must be reconsidered.
9. **Targeted recheck.** Recheck prior findings and changed joins only. Two
   repair cycles is the normal ceiling; a third surviving cycle returns the
   problem to architecture.
10. **Close and advance.** Update canon, allowance rulings, state, registry,
    MICE threads, plant/payoff map, and consequences before the next movement.

## Cadence and scale

Across rolling two-chapter spans, aim for four organic humor or warmth
opportunities; three to five is the working band. A deliberately cold movement
may carry less when declared in advance.

- `MINOR`: normally 600–1,000 words of embodied pressure.
- `MAJOR`: normally 1,500–2,500 words; a chapter containing it is normally
  planned at 6,000–6,500 words when ordinary chapters are near 5,000.
- `CLIMAX`: may span scenes or chapters for escalation, choice, solution, cost,
  and aftermath.

These are functional bands, not padding targets. Do not compress a scheduled
major sequence to protect an arbitrary chapter length.

## Larger passes

At act boundaries, read continuously for inquiry debt, causality, escalation,
relationship motion, action/discovery rhythm, and repeated problem scaffolds.
At book lock run, in order: structural continuity, voice and character
distinction, line edit, and listening proof. A later pass does not quietly redo
an earlier one.

## Typical use in a twenty-chapter book

Expect five movements of four chapters, twenty light closes, five batch editor
runs, five Reader A passes, three to five Reader B passes, audits only for the
scheduled pressure set pieces or major resolutions, zero to three independent
verifier runs, three to five repair/recheck cycles, and four whole-book passes.
These are planning estimates, not quotas.

## Exception lane

Use the single-chapter editor immediately only for a major canon ruling,
irreversible death, new speculative-law reveal, safety-critical real-world
claim, or uncertainty that makes drafting the next chapter unsafe.
