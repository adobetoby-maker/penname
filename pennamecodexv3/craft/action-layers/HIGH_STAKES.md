# Action layer — High Stakes

Use for climaxes, rescues, survival decisions, and fights with irreversible cost.

- Put one concrete failure condition on the page before acceleration.
- Split the objective when possible: winning and protecting what matters pull in
  different directions.
- Show the clock through changing conditions rather than repeated reminders.
- Escalate by removing safe options, degrading resources, or forcing a value choice.
- The decisive act reveals priority or character rather than merely unlocking a
  larger power.
- Do not restore the pre-fight state. Preserve cost in body, trust, knowledge,
  obligation, equipment, or the next problem.
