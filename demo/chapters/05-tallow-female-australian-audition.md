# Tallow Gate Voice Audition — Australian Female

## What you are hearing

This is the opening 240-word scene from **Tallow Gate, Chapter 1**. It is the
same text used in the English female audition, with the same chunk size and the
same 340 ms baseline pause setting.

The voice was designed locally as a mature Australian female audiobook
narrator: warm, grounded, low-to-mid register, subtle natural accent, clear
consonants, intimate microphone presence, understated dry humour, and controlled
emotion. The designed sample was then locked as the reference voice and used by
the local Qwen voice-cloning model to read the passage.

This is a **neutral voice-identity test**, not the final performed edition. It
does not yet contain whole-chapter acting direction, scene-specific pauses,
character casting, music, or sound effects.

## Listen for

- Does the narrator sound like someone you would willingly hear for ten hours?
- Does the Australian accent feel natural rather than performed?
- Does the voice stay like the same person from sentence to sentence?
- Are Toren and Dessa distinguishable without the narrator becoming theatrical?
- Do humour, worry, and Senna's cough register even with restrained delivery?
- Are the sentence endings and pauses comfortable, or does the reading hurry?
- Does the pitch have enough movement without becoming sing-song?
- Does anything about the voice become tiring, breathy, sharp, or artificial?

## What is being decided

Choose the **primary narrator identity** only. Performance intensity, longer
dramatic holds, pitch shaping, and a possible character cast will be tested
after the narrator is chosen.
