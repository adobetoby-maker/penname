He saw it make itself.

It was not there—and then bright;

a line of white,

going out past his knuckles,

finding the length first,

and the shape after.

Then the edges came—

and they came fast—

arriving along it,

from the fist outward;

the way frost goes across a puddle.

And they came up true and thin and matched—

one,

and then the other;

and the blunt, hooked end,

that had been on the thing since the road cut,

arrived last of all—

and arrived straight.

It committed.

He felt the moment—

it decided what it was going to be;

the way you feel the last of a knot go,

when you pull it.

And the whole of his forearm—

went to light.
