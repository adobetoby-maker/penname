# Editor Verdicts — Proof of Craft

Two shakedown tests, both reviewed by GPT (gpt-5.6-sol, high reasoning, via
Codex CLI) — a different model family from the author seat, on purpose, so
the editor doesn't share the drafter's blind spots. Full transcripts live in
`fractured-path/tests/shakedown/reviews/` (not copied into this repo); this
file summarizes the verdicts truthfully.

## Test 1 — Blind A/B: The Exhibition

Two versions of the same fight scene (Cael, 14, unranked, loses to Feryn, a
touring Bronze Rank 2) were handed to the editor **blind**, no origin labels:
**V1** the scene as published (Book 1, Ch. 17), **V2** a harness draft
written from a facts-only card — the author seat never saw the original.

V1 failed 5 combat/craft gates HIGH: no drawable geography, combat delivered
as recap rather than dramatized, no concrete stake, a climax that doesn't
accelerate, a loss never metabolized on the page. V2 passed all five.

Verdict, verbatim: *"Version 2 fulfills the charter substantially better...
The publishable-scene verdict does not differ: Version 2 is stronger."* V1
has cleaner brevity; its fight is fundamentally summarized. V2 still needs a
real line edit — and got one: **9 logged defects** of its own, including two
real production bugs (leftover authoring metadata that should never ship in
a manuscript, and an unseeded supernatural complication). The harness draft
won blind, on both charter and publishability, and still walked away with a
real defect list rather than a clean bill.

## Test 2 — The Silver Standard: the Daeva match

A second harness draft (Cael vs. Daeva, a Gold-tier prodigy) was reviewed
against full canon — bible, state ledger, name registry. Not blind this
time: a real editorial pass.

**Verdict: STRUCTURAL_HOLD.** 12 defects — **2 CRITICAL, 6 MAJOR, 3
MODERATE, 1 MINOR.** Both critical findings are canon violations the editor
caught unprompted: an ability the draft grants Cael that contradicts the
series' locked pre-existing state, and a scoring mechanism whose own stated
rules can't logically produce the ending the scene needs. MAJOR findings
include integration described as copying rather than the bible's
native-digestion mechanic, a foreshadowing-gate failure, and meaning
compressed through summary instead of dramatized.

Editor's own words: *"The scene has a strong central image... It cannot pass
while the loadout violates continuity... and the scoring mechanism cannot
logically produce its required ending."*

**This draft ships unrepaired, by design.** The point isn't a clean scene —
it's proof the editor holds a draft that reads well on the surface and
catches canon violations a drafter would otherwise ship. A repaired version
would prove less than an honest hold.

## What the two tests show together

Test 1: the harness can outscore an already-published, professionally-edited
chapter on a blind read, and still hand back real defects. Test 2: the
editor isn't a rubber stamp — it holds a draft structurally and catches the
kind of canon violation a drafter or reader can miss. Neither test is staged
clean. Both ship as receipts.
